# Задание: позы Аксико, второй набор

The attached image is the approved design of Axiko. Draw the SAME character in new poses: same
head shape, proportions, line weight, eyes, gill shapes (three separate feathery fronds on each
side of the head) and the same white sticker border around the silhouette.

These pictures will be traced into vector shapes, so:

- **Perfectly flat colours only** — no gradients, no shading, no glow, no texture, no highlights
  except the white dot in each eye.
- Use exactly these colours: outline and eyes `#44308A`, body `#C9B6FD`, gills, tail fin edge and
  tongue `#F98FAC`, sticker border pure white `#FFFFFF`.
- Background: one flat colour `#E9E2DC`, nothing else on it.
- Each image 1:1, one sticker, centered, filling about 80% of the square. No text or letters.
- Every pink gill frond must be a separate shape, fully enclosed by the outline.
- Props are flat too, outlined like the character.

Use your image generation tool to create the images below as SEPARATE images, in order. Do not
write code and do not move or save files yourself. In your final message list them in order with
the full path of each generated PNG.

1. **peek** — peeking over the top of a wall, seen from the front: only the head (down to just
   below the smiling mouth), all six gill fronds, and two paws whose fingers rest on the top of the
   wall, one on each side of the chin. Do NOT draw the wall or the body: the sticker ends at the
   bottom in one perfectly straight horizontal line across its full width, right under the paws.
   Curious happy eyes looking at the viewer.
2. **point** — full body, standing, one arm stretched out to the upper right pointing with one
   finger, looking where it points, happy open mouth.
3. **working** — full body, holding a small wrench in one raised paw, focused happy face, tongue
   slightly out at the corner of the mouth. Wrench: flat grey `#9C98A8` with darker `#7D798A` shading
   as a second flat shape.
4. **searching** — full body, holding a magnifying glass up to one eye, that eye looks bigger
   through the lens; the lens is flat pale blue `#CFE8FF`, the rim and handle flat amber `#F5B731`.
5. **asking** — full body, head tilted, one paw raised palm up, small closed mouth; one big flat
   amber `#F5B731` question mark floating beside the head inside its own white sticker border.
6. **love** — full body, hugging a big flat heart `#FF6F91` with both paws in front of the chest,
   eyes closed happily (two upward arcs), blissful smile.
7. **plan** — full body, holding a clipboard in both paws and looking at the viewer: board flat
   amber `#F5B731`, paper flat white with three short flat grey `#9C98A8` lines and one mint
   `#5FB9A0` check mark.
