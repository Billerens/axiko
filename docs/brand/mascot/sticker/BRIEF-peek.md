# Задание: Axiko выглядывает из-за края — два слоя

The attached image is the approved design of Axiko. These two pictures are two LAYERS of one
animation: the character climbs up from behind the bottom edge of a phone screen, holding on to the
edge with its paws. The paws are drawn separately (they stay on the edge), the body separately (it
rises behind the edge). Same character: head shape, proportions, line weight, eyes, gill shapes
(three separate feathery fronds on each side of the head), white sticker border.

Rules for both pictures (they will be traced into vector shapes):

- **Perfectly flat colours only** — no gradients, shading, glow or texture; the only highlight is
  the white dot in each eye.
- Exactly these colours: outline and eyes `#44308A`, body `#C9B6FD`, gills and tongue `#F98FAC`,
  sticker border pure white `#FFFFFF`. Background: one flat colour `#E9E2DC`.
- 1:1 image, no text, no letters, no wall, no ground, no shadow.

Use your image generation tool to create the two images as SEPARATE images, in this order. Do not
write code and do not move or save files yourself. In your final message list them in order with
the full path of each generated PNG.

1. **peek-body** — the character seen from the front, drawn from the top of the gills down to the
   middle of the belly: head with all six gill fronds, happy open mouth with the tongue, looking at
   the viewer, neck and upper body. **No arms and no paws at all** — the sides of the body are
   plain. The sticker ends at the bottom in one perfectly straight horizontal line (the body is cut
   off flat, as if by the edge of the picture). Centered, filling about 80% of the width.
2. **peek-paws** — ONLY two little paws of the same character, nothing else: seen from the front,
   gripping over the top of an edge, so each paw is a small rounded hand with four short fingers
   pointing DOWN. Body colour `#C9B6FD` with the `#44308A` outline. The two paws are two separate
   stickers (each with its own white border), side by side on one horizontal line, with a gap
   between them about three paw-widths wide. Each paw has a flat straight top edge. Centered, the
   pair filling about 60% of the image width.
