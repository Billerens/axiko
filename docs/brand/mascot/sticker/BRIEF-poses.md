# Задание: позы Axiko для векторизации

The attached image is the approved design of Axiko. Draw the SAME character in new poses: same
head shape, proportions, line weight, eyes, gill shapes (three separate feathery fronds on each
side of the head) and the same white sticker border around the silhouette.

These pictures will be traced into vector shapes, so:

- **Perfectly flat colours only** — no gradients, no shading, no glow, no texture, no highlights
  except the white dot in each eye.
- Use exactly these colours: outline and eyes `#44308A`, body `#C9B6FD`, gills, tail fin edge and
  tongue `#F98FAC`, sticker border pure white `#FFFFFF`.
- Background: one flat colour `#E9E2DC`, nothing else on it.
- Each image 1:1, one sticker, centered, filling about 80% of the square. No text or letters.
- Every pink gill frond must be a separate shape, fully enclosed by the outline.

Use your image generation tool to create the images below as SEPARATE images, in order. Do not
write code and do not move or save files yourself. In your final message list them in order with
the full path of each generated PNG.

1. **idle** — head, shoulders and both paws resting in front; calm closed-mouth smile, looking at
   the viewer.
2. **listening** — head tilted, one paw raised beside the gills as if cupping an ear, gills perked
   up, mouth slightly open; three short flat amber `#F5B731` dashes near the raised paw (inside
   their own white sticker border).
3. **thinking** — eyes looking up, one paw on the chin, small closed mouth. Gills plain pink.
4. **building** — holding a small cube in both paws in front of the chest, proud smile; the cube is
   flat mint: top `#8FD9C2`, left side `#5FB9A0`, right side `#3F9A82`, outlined like the character.
5. **sleeping** — curled up asleep, tail wrapped in front, eyes closed (two downward arcs), gills
   relaxed; two small flat squiggles `#A99BF0` floating above the head.
6. **oops** — worried: eyebrows up, small open frown, gills drooping down, one paw on the cheek.
7. **celebrating** — both paws up in the air, eyes closed happily (two upward arcs), big open
   smile; three flat amber `#F5B731` four-point sparkles above.
8. **safe mode** — sitting calmly on a few round flat grey pebbles (`#9C98A8` and `#7D798A`), eyes
   half closed, gentle smile, gills lowered.
9. **name tag** — full body, standing, holding a blank flat white rounded name tag with both paws
   in front of the belly, curious open smile.
10. **head** — only the head with all six gill fronds, facing straight at the viewer, symmetrical,
    open happy mouth with the tongue; no body.
