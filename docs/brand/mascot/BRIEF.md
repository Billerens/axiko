## The character

Axiko is the mascot of a personal Android app that rebuilds itself on request: a small, chubby,
friendly axolotl who lives in the owner's phone and reshapes the app for them. An ORIGINAL
character — it must not resemble any existing mascot or game character (no Pokémon, no Minecraft
look).

- **Body:** soft lavender, light `#C9C0FF` with shading toward `#8B7CFF` (the app's accent).
  Round wide head, short plump body, stubby four-fingered paws, short tail with a soft fin.
- **Gills:** three feathery fronds on each side of the head, warm pink `#F08FA9` with lighter tips.
  The gills are its most expressive part: they perk up, droop, and their tips can light up.
- **Face:** small glossy dark eyes `#2B2433` with a white highlight, a tiny gentle smile, pink
  blush on the cheeks. Big head, small body — cute, never creepy, no teeth.
- **Belly:** paler, almost white lavender `#ECE8FF`.
- **Style:** flat vector-style illustration with soft shapes and very subtle shading, clean
  silhouette, no hard black outlines, no 3D render, no photorealism. Must read well on a dark
  background `#101010` and on white.
- **No text, letters or logos inside any image.**
