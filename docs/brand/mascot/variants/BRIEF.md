# Задание: варианты маскота Аксико

Use your image generation tool to create EIGHT separate images, one per variant below, in this
order. Do not write code and do not try to move or save files yourself — just generate. In your
final message list the variants in order, each with the full path of the generated PNG.

All variants are the same idea — Axiko, the mascot of a personal Android app that rebuilds itself
on request: a small friendly axolotl who lives in the owner's phone. ORIGINAL character, must not
resemble any existing mascot or game character (no Pokémon, no Minecraft look). Each image: 1:1
square, the character alone, full body, front or slight three-quarter view, centered, on a plain
very light warm background `#FBF8F5`. No text, letters or logos. Three feathery gill fronds on each
side of the head in every variant. The app's brand color is violet `#8B7CFF` / `#5B4BDB`.

1. **Mochi** — almost perfectly round, like a soft rice cake; tiny stubby paws, no visible neck,
   lavender body `#C9C0FF`, pink gills `#F08FA9`, flat vector style with very subtle shading.
2. **Swimmer** — closer to a real axolotl: longer body, long tail with a fin, floating mid-swim,
   lavender body, pink gills, flat vector style. Still cute, big eyes.
3. **Classic pink** — the natural pink axolotl body `#F6B8C8` with violet gills `#8B7CFF`, chubby
   sitting pose, flat vector style.
4. **Night violet** — deep violet body `#5B4BDB` with glowing warm coral gills `#FF8A80`, light
   lavender belly, bright glossy eyes; bold, made for dark backgrounds — but show it on the light one.
5. **Sticker** — sticker-pack style: thick soft dark-violet outline `#3B2F8F`, flat fills, white
   sticker border around the silhouette, lavender body, pink gills, playful waving pose.
6. **Plush** — a soft felt plush toy: visible fabric texture and seams, embroidered eyes, lavender
   body, pink felt gills; looks huggable.
7. **Minimal** — built from a few simple geometric shapes (circle head, rounded gill strokes, dots
   for eyes); two colors only, lavender `#B9AEFF` and pink `#F08FA9`, plus dark eyes. Icon-first.
8. **Little helper** — chubby lavender axolotl with pink gills wearing a tiny mint `#5FB9A0` tool
   belt with a little wrench and a paintbrush, proud cheerful pose; flat vector style.
